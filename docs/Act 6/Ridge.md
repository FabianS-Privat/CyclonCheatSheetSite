## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
follow the Right Wall from the Start
:::

Ridge has 2 Variants, one where you enter the Zone from the East Corner and one from the South-West Corner.

You can directly tell which variant you are in by the position of the Waypoint to the Zone Entrance.

The East Variant has the Waypoint West of the Entrance and the West Variant East of the Entrance.

In the East Variant follow the Zone till the Zone curves West. From now till you hit the West Edge of the Zone. Indicated by a curve North. Move West, ignore all North or South Entrances.
Same for the West Variant, just that you follow the Zone East after the first Curve East.
You know you are walking the correct Passage when you move past Wheels on top of Spikes.
