## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Follow the Road for the Exit to Western Forest  
Abberath Arena is in the Sidezone behind the Ledge  
:::

Prisoner's Gate in Part 2 has a massivly reduced amount of Variants compared to it's Part 1 Version.

Follow the Road till you see which direction has the Ledge, then head over the Ledge and find the Passage into the Side Zone. The Entrance to the Abberath Arena is always on the upper Part of the Sidezone. Chekc left of the Passage first, if it is not there check Right of it. (Good Example is Image 4)  
After exiting the Boss Arena, leave the Sidezone and then move in a direct Path into the Direction the Road took.
