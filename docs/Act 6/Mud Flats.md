## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
The Dishonoured Queen is always at the East Edge of the Zone at the former Submerged Passage entrance.  
Move East till you hit the Edge, then South/North to find the Submerged Entrance, highlighted by Nessa at the end of the Submerged Entrance.  
We do not need to talk to Nessa, we just use her for orientation. Kill the Dishonoured Queen.
From the follow the North Edge East till you find the Entrance to Karui Fortress.
:::

Mud Flats got a lot more simplified from its Act 1 Version. We only need to differentiate between East and North Varians.
As we can apply the Simple Read to the East and South Variant.

The North Variant we can still identify the same as in Act 1 Versions. We always spawn with the Rock above the Entrance and past it is open Zone.
For there move North-East till you hit the Round Rock. At the Rock move North to find the Dishonoured Queen.  
After killing the Queen move South-West to find the Entrance to Karui Fortress.
