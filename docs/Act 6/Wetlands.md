## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Follow the Left Wall till you find the Boss Arena Entrance
:::

If you follow the West Edge, you will find a broken Waypoint and after the Boss Arena at some point.
However sometimes the Boss Arena is at the North Edge, this Variant can be identified by the River forming the South Edge extending North while curving East.

Very rarely it is on the East Edge, I have not found a specific Identifier for this Variant.
