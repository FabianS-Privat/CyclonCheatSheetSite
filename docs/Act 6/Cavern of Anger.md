## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Follow along the East Edge
:::

Cavern of Anger in Act 2 has 3 distinguishable Layouts.

- If the Zone extends East, stick to the South Edge and move South-East along it.
- If the Zone extends North, check the Stairs room you have to walk through. If the stairs face West, the Exit is North of the Stairs Room. Move North-East till you see the big open Room, then North.
- Otherwise, if the Stairs face East, move North into North East to find the Exit in the North-East Corner.
