## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Follow the Right Wall from the Waypoint till you find the Trial.  
After the Trial either backtrack back to the Exit to Shavronne's Tower  
or continue to follow the right wall if you have not found the Exit yet.
:::

Lower Prison is the first Zone that deviates from mirroring its Act 1 Version.
While we do have a Trial of Ascendancy here and always check the East Corner first, we have to read the Zone diffrently.

Check the Zone for the following Identifiers:

- You can see the Exit to Shavronne's Tower from the Entrance and Waypoint. We are in the Loop Layout and will find the Trial before the Exit.
- You see the Lever from the Trial through the Wall when we move South, we need to move North-West till we find a Passage leading South, walk past the Exit to Shavronne's Tower to find the Trial.
- The Zone continues East after the first Room East of the Waypoint, follow the North Edge to find the Trial,
- - If the Trial is in a Small Room with no further Exit, backtrack till you find a Passage leading further East, Exit is in the East Corner.
- - If the Room with the Trial Entrance continues East, it connects to the Exit in the East Corner.
- No identifiers, Trial is in the South Corner, the Zone will either Split of East or West. The Exit to Shavronne's Tower is in the Split of Direction.
- - Further identifiable by the direction of the Trial Entrance. If Trial is facing North or East the Exit is East. Otherwise its West.
