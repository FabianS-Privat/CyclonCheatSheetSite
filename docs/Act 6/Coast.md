## Zone Read

While not exactly the same behaviour as the [Act 1 Coast](../Act%201/Coast.md). The major rules of the Zone still apply.
The Exits and Waypoint are on the Base Layer of the Zone and both _Handgun_ and _Long coast_ Variants still exist.

The major difference to Act 1 is that we now have both Movement Speed and Movement Skills available to us. Shortening the way we run the Zone.

Identify the direction the Zone extends and outside of the special Variants, move in a direct Line in that direction.
