## Zone Read

Solaris Temple 2 has diffrent Layouts in Part 2 compared to Part 1. We always head South at the Start and depending on the Shape and Connections the Room below the Starting Room has we know which direction the Zone extends to.

- If the Room continues West or has a Door leading West, it is a West Layout. Follow the North Edge West till you find the Final Room, where Diala waited in Act 3.

- Otherwise you have a East Variant, follow the North Edge East till you can move North.
- - Rarely it is the Loop Variant, where you have have to follow the Zone further West after moving North before you see the final Room. You can notice this Layout when you move North and aren't in the final Room already.
