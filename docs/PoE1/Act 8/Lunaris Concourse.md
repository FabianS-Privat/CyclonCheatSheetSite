### Point of Interest

## Zone Read

Lunaris Concourse is following the same Static Layout Rules as Ebony Barracks in Act 3. The only Exception is the diffrence in Entrance / Exits. Lunaris Concourse has 2 additional Exits, but the Imperial Garden Exit is overgrown and removed.

You enter the Zone from the Bathhouse Entrance, the Waypoint is North-East of the Entrance.
From the Waypoint the head North-West for Lunaris Temple Entrance. The Exit can either face North or East. You can see the Door Icon on the Minimap when aproaching it.

The Entrance to the Harbour Bridge is South-East of the Waypoint.
