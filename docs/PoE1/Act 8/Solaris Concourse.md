## Zone Read

Underbelly places us at a _random_ Place on the Base Layer of Solaris Concourse. Solaris Concourse has the same Layout and Variants as Battlefront in Act 3.
The first step is to find anything to orient with. Move east to check if you are in the Dock Spawn Variant,

- if you see a massive Wall you can Blink Arrow / Lightning Warp / Leap Slam up. Move North from there to find the Exit.
- if you see a Edge (Housewall), backtrack until you can move North, then move North till you find the Solaris Temple Entrance.
- if you find neither Wall or Edge, move North till you see the Ledge, from there move further North till you see the River. Follow the River to the Fountain, then move behind the Fountain for the Solaris Temple Entrance.
