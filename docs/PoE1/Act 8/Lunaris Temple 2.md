### Zone Read

Lunaris Temple 2 of Part 2 Act 8 follows the same Layout Rules as Lunaris Temple 2 of Part 1 Act 3 with the Exception that the South-West Layout either does not exist or is to rare for me to find.

The first Part of Lunaris Temple 2 is a Maze created by a series of Bridges.  
There are 3 notable Dead End Rooms, _Square_, _Rectangle_, and _Double Platform_. It is recommended to familirize with their Shapes on the Minimap to identify early and avoid.

The Maze has 3 Layers. connected by 3 long staircases. The first and second Layer are always connected by 2 Staircases. So the first Stair does not determine the Layout Direction yet. So to traverse the first and second Layer of the Maze take one Direction and search for the Staircase. IF you find it take the Staircase, otherwise change direction and repeat. Once you have found the 2nd Staircase, the third one will be facing the same Direction as the 2nd one.

:::tip Cyclon's Note
You can go clockwise our counterlclockwise, just always stick to the same Pattern.
:::

### Zone Read

The second Part of Lunaris Temple is semi-static.
It consist of a long Pathway, 2 big Gateways, a Cage Maze and a final Walkway leading to the Piety Fight.

For the first long Pathway look at the Flower Pots right next to the 3rd Staircase, you can determine the Correct direction based on which side only has **one** Flower Pot.
If you have a North Layout, the Path will be one Long L Piece, with 1 Turn before you reach the first Gate.
The second Gate in a North Layout will always be in a direct Line from the first Gate. After that follow the Walkway 2 Stairs up to find the Boss Arena.

On a South Variant, the Path will take few more turns, but will also end on the first Gate.
After the First Gate the 2nd Gate will be in a adjacent Direction from it. The rooms leading up to the 2nd Gate will force the Player to walk in a U-Pattern.
If the Stairs in the first Part faced East, then the 2nd Gate will also face East, so move East on the U-Pattern.
