## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Follow along the Left Edge (Waterside) from the Start
:::

Quay's North Edge is made of Housewalls or Rubble while it's South Edge is the Waterside. In the first Section of the Zone we want to stick to the Waterside and move along the Direction the Zone extends to.  
This gurantees us to find the Ankh in the Sealed Casekt. Whenever the Zone splits we choose the West Option, if no West Option exist the South Option until we find the Resurrection Side.  
After the Resurrection Side we have to backtrack to the last Split and chose the other Route, once you see the Housewalls again stick to the North Edge till you find the Grain Gate Entrance.
