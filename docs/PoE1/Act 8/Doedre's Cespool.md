### Point of Interest

Waypoint (after Doedre Kill)

## Zone Read

Doedre's Cespool has 3 Variants, we can identify the Variant we are in after 1-3 Rooms.  
Always go across the first Bridge then go West into the Curve.

- If there is a Room splitting of West of that Room, it is the West Variant, stick to the West Edge of teh Zone and move South till you see the Room with the Arena Entrance. It has always the same Shape, just a diffrent orientation.
- Otherwise head into the next Room.
- - If you can continue South along the West Edge, it is the South Variant.
- - Otherwise the Zone forces you to move east and it is the East Variant.

With sticking to the West Edge we have a longer Pathing in the East Variant, but better Pathing in the South and West Variant.
