Waypoint

## Zone Read

Normally we enter the Zone through Imperial Field from the East, however we enter it from the West due to new Pathing.

A Side Effect is a simplified Pathing through Solaris Temple 1.

We move East from the Entrance till we see the Waypoint.

- If the Waypoint is in the West Corner of the Room, move North behind the Waypoint to see the Exit to Solaris Temple 2.
- If the Waypoint is in the East Corner, move North till you get into a Room splitting of West, take West there to find the Solaris Temple 2 Entrance.
