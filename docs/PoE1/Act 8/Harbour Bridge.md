### Point of Interest

Statue of the Sisters, starts the Bossfight

## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Move North-East in the middle of the Bridge till the Door to the Boss Arena
:::

Harbour Bridge is a Linear Static Layout, the South Side is in Nighttime, while the Northside is in Daytime. Move North-East from the Lunaris Concourse Entrance till you see the Door Icon on the Minimap or Night turns to Day, enter the Boss Arena.
