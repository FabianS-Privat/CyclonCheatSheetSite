## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Follow the Road.  
From WP follow Road with Wagons for Tower  
and North Corner (ofroad) for Trial
:::

Follow the Road till you reach the Waypoint.  
From there The Library is always North-West. Follow the Road in that Direction to find the Library Entrance.  
The Entrance to the Sceptre of God is North-East or East of the Waypoint. Follow the Series of Wagons to find the Entrance.
The Trial is in the North Corner of the Zone, there is no Road leading to the Trial, however sometimes the Trial Passage connects to either the Library Path or Splits of from the Wagon Road.

Always move North from the Waypoint, if it overlaps with the Wagon Road, take the first Big Passage that splits of the Road. From theere move North if you can, otherwise move East to find the Trial of Ascendancy.
