## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
After the Starting Room: Move East if you can, move North otherwise.
:::

The Starting Room can face East or West, move throuhg and find the exit.
The Trial has to extend North or East. If we always move with that in Mind in the direction the Zone Extends. We can spot the Trial fairly quickly.
Follow the rules of the Simple Read. If you can move East, move east and if you can't move East, but can move North move North.
