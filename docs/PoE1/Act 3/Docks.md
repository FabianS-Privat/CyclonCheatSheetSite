## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
You spawn close to a Corner, follow the other Direction of the Wall till you findthe Thaumatic Dust.
:::

The Waypoint, Quest Item and Essence Boss spawn in a Random Corner the Zone. The best way to minimize Time spend in this Zone is to identify the Zone Variant and follow the Correct Edge.

If it is the South Variant, follow the Right Wall from the Entrance South.
If it is the North Variant, follow the Left Wall from the Entrance North.
