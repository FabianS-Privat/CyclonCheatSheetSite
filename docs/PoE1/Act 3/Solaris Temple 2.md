## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Follow the Red Carpet
:::

Solaris Temple 2 has 6 Variants, 4 Skipless and 2 where a small Variation creates a Skip.

At the Start always move West to check for the Small Skip.

If you can dash across the Gap you are in the Small Skip Nort Variant. Follow the Passage North till it curves East, then follow the Zone east till it curves North again. Move North to find Dialla.

Otherwise Head into the Direction the Zone is extending to, either North or South. The Variant can be determined once reaching the next Corner.

- If the Zone extends North it is either the West Layout or the North Layout. When able to move East. It is either a Dead End and you are in the West Variant. Follow the Zone West along the North Edge. Otherwise it extends East and you can follow the Zone North along the East Edge

- If the Zone extends South it is either the East or South Layout. The South Layout does not curve East. So if you have a forced Pathing East you know you are in the East Layout. Follow the Zone east along the East Edge. Otherwise follow the Zone South. It will do a U-Turn before you can see the Dialla Area.  
  If you can see Dialla or the Book Icon on your Minimap while moving South the South Skip is available. To do the Skip stand at the West Edge and use Blink Arrow / Lightning Warp to dash across the Gap.
