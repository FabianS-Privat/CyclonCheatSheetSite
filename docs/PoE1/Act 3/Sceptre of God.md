## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Traverse the Zone Clockwise and stay in the inner Square of the Zone
:::

Sceptre of God consist of 3 Floors, the Exit is always in one of the Corners, on the first Floor it has to be in the North-East or South-East Corner.'
After that it can be in any Corner, except the one the Entrance to that Floor is in. Choose a Direction and check the first Corner.
Stay in the inner Area of the Zone, if you aproach a Corner and do not get the Door Icon on the Minimap, the Staircaise to the next Floor won't be in that Corner. Check the Corner Clockwise or Counterclockwise

The final Floor has both a Waypoint and the Staircase to the Upper Sceptre of God in its Corners.

:::info Cyclon's Note
My 'vibe' Read of the Zone is to always move into the direction the Stairs places me, this works often enough that it feels correct. Exception is that the Direction I placed in does not have a Door, then I check the other Direction first.
:::
