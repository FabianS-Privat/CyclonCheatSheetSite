### Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Move North-West for the Waypoint. Waypoint is on the First Elevation Layer. Docks Entrance is North of Waypoint and Solaris Temple Entrance is at the end of the River going through the Zone.
:::

The Zone has some set Rules it followes. It always has 3 Layers, The Entrance from Marketplace and Exit to Docks is always on the Ground Layer. The Waypoint is always on the 1st Layer and the Entrance to Solaris Temple is always on the 2nd Layer. Additionaly the Solaris Temple also has a diffrent Floor Tile. Through the Zone goes a River you can either Dash across with Level 10 Movement Skills or find a Bridge to get across. The River originates from a Solaris Statue Fountain. The Statue always points directly at the Solaris Temple Entrance. Lastly the River will always go between the Entrance from Marketplace and the Waypoint.

Given this Ruleset for the Zone. We can very easily navigate it. First move North-East to find both the River, to cross it, and the 1st Elevation Layer to move up. Once you are on the first Layer find the Waypoint and Ribon Spool. From there we know Docks Exit is North to North-West. The Difference here is that on the North-West Variant, we find the North Edge very quickly. Move North first and if you get to the Edge, move East to find Docks, otherwise it will be in a direct Line from the Waypoint.
When coming back into Battlefronst to find Solaris Temple 1. Follow the River opposed to its Flow to find the Statue and move from there to Solaris Temple 1 Entrance.
