### Zone Read

On Right Facing Variants it would be slightly faster to not take the Goat Passage, however it is highly Recommended to always take the Goad Passage for it excelent Source of Experience.
During the Goat Passage, you will get near a Campfire, once you get in close Range, The Faun will drop and kill all Goats surounding the Campfire.

So the way to follow the Zone always stick to the Mountain Side Wall till you get out of the Goat Passage, then stick to the Water Side Wall to avoid the AoE Explosion of the Totem Boss, Kuduku the False God.
