## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read

Follow the Cliff Side till you see the Ship Graveyard Cave Entrance or the Cavern of Wrath Entrance.
If you see the Ship Graveyard Cave, place a Portal near it and search for Cavern of Wrath.
If you find Cavern of Wrath first, enter, get WP, exit and search for Ship Graveyard Cave.

:::

Fairgraves will always spawn near the Waypoint, you can also see a Lore Interaction near Fairgraves Arena, exiting from the Ship Graveyard Cave Exit behind the Allflame, will place you at Fairgraves Arena.

The Ship Graveyard Cave Entrance can be anywhere in the Zone, however it is usualy close to the Edge. In 9/10 cases it is near the Cliff Side. Rarely it is near the Coast Side.
In most cases you will find the Ship Graveyard Cave before finding the Entrance to Merveils Cavern.
Rarely it is after Merveils Cavern in your pathing following the Cliff Side.

To find the Ship Graveyard Cave and Cavern of Wrath efficiently. Follow the Cliffside Wall of the Zone.

There are 3 Layout Groups. It is neat to identify what Variant you are in, to make it easier to understand where the Exits are gona be.

South Extending Zones, which place you facing North, but extend South. The Waypoint is usualy directly visible in these Zones and you can head South paralel to the Cliffside almost imeadiatly. See Examples in South Extending Zone Column.

North-East Entrance Variants, placing the Character facing North-East and North-West Variants placing the Character facing North-West.

If the Ship Graveyard Cave has not been discovered by the time you found the Cavern of Wrath. Get the Waypoint from Cavern of Wrath, then head out. In North-East Variants the Ship Graveyard Cave Entrance is likely along the Cliff Side, but in North-West Variants, expect the Entrance to Ship Graveyard Cave to be Southish.
