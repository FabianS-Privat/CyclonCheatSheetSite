### Zone Read

The Allflame and Exit to Fairgraves Arena are always in a Diagonal Line to the Entrance. The Pathing through the Cave System is a bit more Zig Zag. But keeping the Diagonal Line in mind we just need to determine in which Direction the Zone extends to.

Based on the First 1-2 Turns we have to take to avoid Dead Ends and take Curves, we can determine which direction the Zone faces.

A special rule specific to the South Variant is that the Allflame Room can never be entered from the North. In the South Variant the entrance will always be from the West.

So with those Rules and Patterns in mind. Take the Direcction the Zone forces you, look for Edges to orient the Layout and avoid Dead Ends by keeping the Diagonal Line from Entrance to Exit in mind.
