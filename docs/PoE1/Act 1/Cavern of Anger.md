## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Follow the Water
:::

There are 2 Variants for Cavern of Anger. The Exit of the Zone is always in the North-East Corner of the Zone. You can identify which one by checking the Upper Bridge at the Start.

- If it leads to a Dead End, you are in the North Variant. Move North-East along the River and the West Edge.

- Otherwise, you are in the East Variant. Move East along the River.
