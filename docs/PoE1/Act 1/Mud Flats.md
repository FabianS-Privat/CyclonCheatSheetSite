## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read

The perfect Read for Mud Flats can be a bit complicated, the simple Rule is that all 3 Nests are in a Trinagle Pattern, so just learn to differentiate between the Major Layout Variants: East, North and South. Then just look for Quest Markers and Exit in the general Direction of the Variant.

:::

There are 3 Variants that we can determine between, based on our Starting location. East, North and South Variants.

If we are facing South and next to the Entrance is a long Wall we have the South Variant, head south till you find the First Nest, from there go east to find Nest 2 and Nest 3. Seen in Image 3

If we are facing South but the Zone opens up both South and North, then we are in a East Variant. We read all East Variants the same, go east and look for Nest 1, from there look for Nest 2 and then one last time for Nest 3. The diffrence in the Variants is only where the exit is, so after nest 3, head further East and keep an eye out for the Exit Quest Marker. If right next to Nest 3 is a Wall and you see the Quest Marker, then follow the Wall sout / east till you find the opening to Submerged.
If there is no Wall next to the 3rd Nest, as in Image 1, head North/East to find the Exit.

The Last Variant, the North one is a bit more Tricky. First we can identify if we are in a North Variant by the Big Rock right above the Entrance. Next The Exit location can be determined based on the distance between the 1st Nest and the Zone entrance. If the 1st Nest is super close and we can not see the 2nd Nest directly. Head North-East for Nest 2 and from Nest 2 head North-West for Nest and the Exit is in the far North-West corner. Layout can be seen in Image 2.

If the 1st Nest is close to the Start and you can see the 2nd Nest Quest Marker at the 1st Nest. Head north for the 2nd Nest, then East for the 3rd and finaly North to find the Exit above Nest 3.

Lastly for the North Exit Variant, the 1st Nest unusualy far away from the Entrance, walk around the Rock above the Entrance. Then head North-East till you see the first Nest Quest Marker. Head to Nest 1, from there head North-East for Nest 2 and finaly North-West again for Nest 3. From Nest 3 head north to find the Exit Quest Marker.
