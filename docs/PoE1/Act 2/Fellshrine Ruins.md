### Points of Interest

3 Stairs, leading to 3 Statues, breaking all 3 will Spawn a Unique Boss before the big Ruin leading to Crypt.

## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Follow the Road
:::

The road will lead through 3 Walls, after the Third, the big Ruin surrounding the Crypt Entrance will appear. Follow the Road till you are at the final Ruin, then head North-East to enter Crypt.
Sometimes the Road splits into two temporarly, when it does make sure to take the Path leading East when it reconnects.

:::tip Cyclon's Tip
Kill the small Insect Groups to keep up your Quicksilver Flask Charges and Frenzy Charges
:::
