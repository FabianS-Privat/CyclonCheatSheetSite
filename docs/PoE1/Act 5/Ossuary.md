## Zone Read

There are 2 Ossuary Variants, both Looping back to the Entrance. To differentiate between the 2. Check North, if the double Bridge is there it is the North Variant. Otherwise you can't move north and the the zone loops South.

Follow along the Zone along the Inner Edge / Wall to find the Tomb of the first Templar.

:::tip Cyclon's Note
After the Tomb you can log/portal if your Loadscreen is fast enough, otherwise use the lever and leave the zone via the entrance.
:::
