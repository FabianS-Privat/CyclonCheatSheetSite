## Zone Read

Torched Courts is a reverse of the Templar Courts. we do want to traverse it a bit diffrently though.
For the first Section the Goal is to move East. Sometimes the Rooms force you to path South, sometimes North to get further East. follow the Rooms till you get to the _Courtroom_.
Now the Goal is to move West. The best way I found is to try and navigate through the Corridors. We move a Corridor West, then a Corridor North and repeat till we find the Exit to Ruined Square.

That the Exit and Entrance are still paralel to each other still aplies.
