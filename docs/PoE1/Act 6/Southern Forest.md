## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Move East till you hit the Big Wall at the East Edge, then South
:::

Southern Forest consist of a Series of Island connected by small Bridges, if you have a Lvl 10 Movement Skill you can skip across from one Island to another (bigger Gaps only with Blink Arrow / Leap Slam / ) without having to find the Bridge.
Once you have reached the Mountainside head South East along the Mountainside. Ignore the first passage North, it would have lead to the former Town Entrance, now blocked. In the South-East Corner of the Zone is the Cavern of Anger Entrance.
