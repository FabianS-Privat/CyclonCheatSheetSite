## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Follow the Left Wall from the Entrance of each Floor
:::

Shavronne's Tower consist of 4 Floors and the Rooftop as the Boss Arena. The Exit of each Floor will always facing North-West or North-East. The final Floor Exit will always be a small Walkway leading to a Ladder.

The Zone is overal linear as in there is usualy just 1 Path to take. However sometimes the Zone fakes with Dead Ends, to avoid those follow the Simple Read for the Ground Floor.

Then from the 2nd Floor onwards, we can identify the correct direction the Exit to the Next Floor has to be.  
The Floors get placed in a Line on the Minimap, the direction the Room is to the previous Room is the Direction the next Exit will be.
For Example if the the 2nd Room is East of the 1st Room. Move East in 2nd + Room.
