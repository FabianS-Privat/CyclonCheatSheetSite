### Point of Interest

Storage Chest

## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Go West, if facing a Ledge, turn around and take the East Side to get to the Storage Chest.
:::

Continuing the Trend Tidal Island too follows the Rules of it's Act 1 Version in a simplified Manner.

We always go West first, turn around on Ledges with same Distance between wall as Width and take Ledges with long Distance to the Wall.
However on Rock Passages I have only found continue Variants.
