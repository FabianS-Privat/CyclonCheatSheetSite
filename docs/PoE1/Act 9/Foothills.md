## Zone Read

Foothills is a Semi Static Layout. The Zone consist of several Layers and always extends North-East. The Waypoint and entrance to Boiling Lake is on the 2nd Layer on the North Edge and the Exit to Tunnel is on the 4th Layer in the North-East Corner.

From the Entrance move North-East onto the 2nd Layer and further North-East to find the Waypoint.
Then from the Waypoint move North-West till you hit the West Edge then further North. If you hit the North Edge, head West along the North Edge.
