## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Stick to the East Edge.  
If you start at the North Corner, move South.  
Otherwise move North
:::

Oasis either extends North,South or West. While it is a big open Zone like Vastiri Desert, the Variant can quickly be identified.

- If you spawn at the East Edge, you are in the West Variant and the Sand Pit is in the West Corner.

Otherwise it is the North or South Zone with a East Edge Spawn.

- If the Zone let's you move North, you are in the North Variant. Otherwise you are at the North Corner and the Sand Pit Boss Arena is in the South Corner.

Once you have identified the direction the Zone extends to head into that direction along the East Edge of the Zone.
