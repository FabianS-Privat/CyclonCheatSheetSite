## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Second Tunnel in Main Room  
Follow the Zone in the Dreams
:::

Rotting Core is 4 Static Layouts packaged into one.  
On the Main Zone move South-West, ignore the first Tunnel North, take the second. The Second Tunnel directly leads to the Entrance to the Core, while the First one curves East first.

Do the Dreams in any Order. The Dreams are linear with no Dead Ends. Shavronne's and Maligaro's Dreams are going North-West, while Doedre's is going North-East. So just follow the Zone in that Direction to find the Boss Rooms.
