## Zone Read

Vastiri Desert is a big open Area with little Indicators. We know that the Storm-Weathered Chest is either North, East, South or West of the Waypoint and we know that the Waypoint is always East of the Entrance.

So we have 3 Objectives when going through Vastiri Desert.

1. Finding the Waypoint
2. Finding the Storm Blade
3. Finding the Exit to Foothills

On the 2nd Entering we have 1-2 Objectives

1. Finding the Storm Blade (if not done already)
2. and want to find Oasis Entrance

Always aim to find the Waypoint first. If you find the Stormblade on the West Spot before the Waypoint, imediatly go for the Exit North of the Waypoint after activating the Waypoint. Otherwise check if the Storm-Weathered Chest is West of the Waypoint.
Move West till you hit the Edge, if no Storm-Weathered Chest, move North-East to diagonal East above the Waypoint, if you hit the Edge again before finding the Chest, the Chest is in the South Corner of the Zone. Otherwise you should see the Quest Indicator for the Storm-Blade.

On the 2nd Entrance to the Zone, if you haven't found the Stormblade yet, move South-West till you see the Quest Indicator and Log / Portal.
Then head North-East to find the Oasis Entrance.
