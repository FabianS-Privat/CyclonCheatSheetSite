## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
The Boss Arena is in the North Corner, follow the East Edge
:::

Refinery has 4 Variants, while the Spawn Room is the same in each Instance, the Outdoor Area surrounding it and the Connection to the next Room is unique to each Variant.

- If the Outdoor Zone has no Connection indoor North or West, but an Entrance directly East then you are in the Small Outdoor Area Variant. Move North-East through the first Indoor Room, in the Outdoor Area directly enter through the opposite Door. Move North-East through the 2nd Indoor Area and in the final Outdoor Area move North-East till you see the Boss Room.

- If the Outdoor Zone has no Connection indoor North or West and is extending East. Follow the Outdoor Area along the North Edge till you see a Entrance, go through the Indoor Area North-East and North East again in the 2nd Outdoor Area to find the Boss Room.

- If the First Zone can be imeaditly exited North you are in the Outer Edge Variant, just follow the West Edge North. In the 2nd Indoor Area move North-East and do the same in the 3rd Outdoor Area.

- Finally if the First Zone can be Excited South-West after moving North, follow the Inner Edge to leave the first Indoor Room and find the Entrance to the 2nd Indoor Room. Leave the 2nd Indoor Zone North-East and move again North-East in the last Outdoor Zone to find the Boss Room.
