## Zone Read

Refinery is mostly a Static Layout, but it has a rare Variant where both the Refinery Exit and the Garukhan Boss Arena are on the same East Edge.

From the Entrance always move North-East to find the Waypoint
from the Waypoint move North-East to find either the Refinery Entrance or Garukhan Boss Arena

- If the Refinery Entrance is East of the Waypoint, the Garukhan Boss Arena is South-West.
- If the Garukhan Boss Arena is East of the Waypoint, the Refinery Entrance is North. Move along the North Edge West till you find the Entrance (after killing Garukhan).
