## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Exit is North East  
Trial is first Tunnel Split East/West
:::

Tunnel is a long Linear Zone. The Trial is always in the First Tunnel splitting of East or West. In 2 Variants you can see the Book Icon from the Trial on the Minimap, but one Variant takes a U-Curve and is a bit further away. So seeing the Book Icon is not a foolproof Indicator.
After the Trial follow the Zone North East till you reach the Exit.
