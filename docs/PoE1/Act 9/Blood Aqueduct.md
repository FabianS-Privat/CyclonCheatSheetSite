## Zone Read

Blood Aqueduct in Part 2 is a Bloody Version of Aqueduct in Part 1 Act 4. Move North-East to find the Town Entrance, if your Path ends there will be a Bridge to the other Walkway acroos the Aqueduct. Repeat till you find the Town Entrance.

The Sidepassages still contain guranteed Magic Packs, which are great for Leveling.
