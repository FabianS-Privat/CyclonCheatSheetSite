## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
The Waypoint is always next to the Passage that leads to Chamber of Sins 2. Find the Entrance to the Inner Circular Room, then head to the Passage behind the Waypoint.
:::

Zone is exactly the same as in Act 2. Just locked Chamber of Sins 2 Entrance

Walk South,East,South to see the Circle Room through the Wall.

- If you see the Waypoint move the opposite Direction and along the inner Wall to find a Passage leading to the Circle Room.
- If you do not see the Waypoint, move East to find the Passage, rarely East is the Dead End.
