## Zone Read

The first Part of this Zone follows the same Rules as it's Act 2 Version.  
Draw the Line and follow the Opposite Wall of the Waypoint, if it is below, and the Wall behind the Waypoint, if the Waypoint is above the Line.

To enter the second Part of the Zone you need to open the Sarcophagus.
The Sarcophagus Room always looks the same, just from a diffrent Angle, you can identify it at the Corner Pattern.
![Sarcophagus Room](./img/Crypt/Sarcophagus%20Room.png)

In the North and East Variant, you walk past the Sarcophagus Room to find the Trial.  
While in the the South and West Variant, you have to continue follow the Wall to find it.

The Container of Sin in the 2nd Part is always in a Diagonal Opposite to the Entrance. Follow one Edge till you hit the Corner, then turn and move in the Direction the Zone continues.
