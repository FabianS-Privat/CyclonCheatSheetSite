## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Move South-West to the West Edge  
Move North along the West Edge  
If you find the Dread Thicket Entrance, place a Portal and continue
:::

While Northern Forest has a few additional Variations, we run them all the same. The Focus is spotting the Dread Thicket, which can spawn in 4 Places.

- Early along the West Edge
- Late along the West Edge
- South-West Corner
- Center of the Zone near the West Edge

From the Waypoint move mostly West, slightly North. If your path West gets blocked, dodge the Terrain South. Once you reached the West Edge move along the West Edge North.
If you see the Entrance or see it on your Minimap place a Portal and continue North till you see the Causeway Exit.
If you have not found the Dread Thicket Entrance at all. It is in the South Corner, reenter the Zone later from the Waypoint and move South.
