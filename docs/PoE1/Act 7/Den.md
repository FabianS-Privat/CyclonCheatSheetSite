## Zone Read

Den has 3 Variants all easily identifiable based on their Starting Directions.

In all Variants follow the direction the Zone extends to and at the first Intersection turn 90° Right and continue in the new Direction.

If the starting Direction is North-West you have to do another 90° Turn, but Left this time. So it would be North-West into North-Est into North West, then you see the Exit.

If the starting Direction is South-East you take another Right Turn. So it would be South-East into South-West into North-West.

And if the starting Direction is South-West you just continue North-West, So its South-West into North-West.
