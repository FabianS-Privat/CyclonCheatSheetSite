## Zone Read

Torched Courts is a reverse Run of its Act 5 Version. The Zone is Semi-Static, the Entrance and Exit are Paralel to each other.

For the First Section avoid Rooms, try to go through the Corridors and move East till you hit the East Edge. Move South to reach the _Courtroom_.  
From the Courtroom start heading West, if the Room you path through only exits North or South, take the South Path and continue West afterwards.  
When reaching the same Height as the Entrance again, head North to find the Exit to Desecrated Chambers.
