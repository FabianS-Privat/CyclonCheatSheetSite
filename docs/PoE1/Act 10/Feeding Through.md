### Point of Interest

Sin, to open Kitava Boss Arena

## Zone Read

Feeding Through is a Static Zone. 2 Walkways next to a Canal filled with Blood and Corpses. Kitava's Boss Arena is always in the North-Corner of the Zone.
Both Walkways can be used to reach the Boss. The West Walkway is recommended as it contains a Crafting Recipe and is closer to Sin. Just walk in a Straight Line North-East.
