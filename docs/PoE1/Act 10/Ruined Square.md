## Zone Read

Ravaged Square is a static Layout, it will always look the same. The Keydifference to its Act 5 Variant Ruined Square is that the North Bridge is broken. (And that you enter the Zone at the Cathedral Rooftop)

The Blood Fountain is always South-East of the Entrance.

The Waypoint is North-East from the Blood Fountain, if you have Blink Arrow / Lightning Warp, you can move North-East and dash over the Broken Bridge. Otherwise head South-East into East to cross the lower Bridge. Then head North till you can move East again to find the Waypoint.

The Torched Courts Entrance is South of the Waypoint in the Snake Pattern, follow the Zone.

Control Blocks Entrance is South-West of the Blood Fountain, move South-West till you find a opening in the Wall made of Houses, head through and repeat one more Time to find the Entrance to Control Blocks.
