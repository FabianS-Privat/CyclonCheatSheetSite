## Zone Read

Kaom's Dream is a _linear_ Zone, as in one Path, though it is a very curved S-shaped Path. The Exception are 1-2 Skips available at the Lava Falls. That said, not every Layout Variant has those available. Most have 1 Skip in the middle of the Zone. Rarely you get the Variant that has 0 Skips and even more rarely one that has 2 Skips.

You can identify the double Skip Layout by its open Starting Area and late Path splitting of to the East.
