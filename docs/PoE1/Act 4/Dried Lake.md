### Point of Interest

Legion Monolith in the middle of the Zone

## Zone Read

The first thing you will see of the Voll Boss Arena is in 9/10 Cases the Book Icon on the Minimap.
Once you see the Icon move in that Direction.

To figure out the general Layout Variant you are in, check the West Wall at the Spawn,

- if you have a long Passage before reaching the Big Pillars, do not walk past them, instead move West to find the Boss Arena.
- if you have a open Zone, but a Big Blockage when walking South-East, walk South along the Hole till you find Voll Arena.
- if you have a open Zone with no Big Hole or Small Passage into open Zone before Pillars, just walk South-East till you find the Book/Voll.
