## Zone Read

Grand Arena is, once understood how it Works, a very simple Layout.

It consists of a Series of Arenas, connected by Corridors. It starts linear, but after Arena 2 there is a Split in the Road. The wrong Path either leads in a Dead End or an additional Arena to path through.

So the way we go through the Zone is as follows:

- Follow the Corridor if it does not split
- In an Arena cross it directly to find the next Corridor Entrance
- When the Plath Splits take the North or West into North Option.

The Final Arena contains 3 Unique Mini-Bosses we have to kill before we can enter the Final Corridor and Boss Arena.
