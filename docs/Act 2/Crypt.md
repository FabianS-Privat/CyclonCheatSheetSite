## Zone Read

Draw a Horizontal Line through the Starting Room, if the Waypoint is below that Line, follow the Opposite Wall of the Waypoint.
If the Waypoint is above the Line, follow the Wall behind the Waypoint.  
That wall will lead to both the Trial and the Entrance to Crypt 2.

If your starting room places you facing South-West, you can either be in the North Exit or West Exit Variant. You can differentiate between the two by the Passage after the Trial. If there is no Passage or the Passage leads West.
You are in the West Exit Variant.
