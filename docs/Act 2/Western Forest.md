## Zone Read

:::tip Simple'Read
Weaver opposite Side of Waypoint from the Road.  
Alira Same Side, a Pebble/ Small Rock Path leads from the Road to Alira.  
Blackguard Camp at the End of the Road.
:::

Follow the Road till you get to the Waypoint.
From there head to the opposite Side of the Road to find Weaver, there are Spiderwebs at the Forest Edge near the Entrance, use these to find it. If you saw Spiderwebs and they stopped, you missed the Entrance.
Alira is on the same Side of the Waypoint, you can follow the road until you see the pebble Path, but it can take longer than just walking to the Edge and running along the Edge till you see Alira or her Arena on your Minimap.
After Alira, walk to the West Edge of the Zone, if you find the road again, follow the Road. If you hit the South Corner, walk North-West to find the Camp.
