## Zone Read

![Secret Door](./img/Caverns/Secret%20Door.png)
Secret Door is always in the Same room with 2 fallen Rubble Walls, click the 2 small Pillars to open the Door.

For the Cave Part, always take the 2 Bridges West, then move South till you hit the Corner turning East. After that move East till you reach the Entrance with the Waypoint.

From there the Exit can be in any of the 3 Corners of the Zone, generaly Recommend starting checking first the East Corner, then North, then West, however sometimes you can see the Door from a Distance like in Image 3.
