## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Follow the Waterside East Edge North
:::

Regardless of Starting Position we walk North into the direction the Zone leads to find the Waypoint. Once we found the Waypoint we use the Terrain around it to determine the Layout.
If the Layout is beneath the Ledge then we follow the East Edge North, **unless** the Water Below is fully surrounding the Waypoint from the South, like in Image 4.

In that case and if the Waypoint is East of the Ledge, we stick to the Western Forest Edge of the Zone till we get close to the Entrance to Cavern. (See Image 2-4)
