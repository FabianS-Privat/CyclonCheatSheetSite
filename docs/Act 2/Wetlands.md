## Zone Read

If you spawn right at the East Edge, the Waypoint will be in the South-West Corner, move North-West to find Oak, from Oak move South and get to the Waypoint.
If your spawn is centered Oak can be either close the West Edge, East Edge or in the Center of the Zone.
We can simplify the location process a lot.

- Move North to find Oak,first check a bit more North-West, if no Oak then check further North-East. Example good to see in Image 1 and 3.

![Oak Arena Indicator](./img/Wetlands/Oak%20Arena%20indicator.png)
You can spot Oak very well by his Arena, it is the only big Structure in the Zone. if you see any Edges with small ZigZags , it will be Oak's Bandit Camp.

- After killing Oak walk East of his Arena Northwards. If you can see the East Edge when walking past Oak's Arena. The Waypoint will be in the North-East Corner.
- If you can't see the East Edge, you are either in the Center Oak or West Edge Oak Variant. In both we walk North into North West till we see the Waypoint.
