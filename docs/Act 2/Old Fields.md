## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read

Follow the Road to get to the Crossroad Entrance.

:::

The Exit to Crossroads will always be at the End of the road. The road can sometimes disapear temporarly. To refind the road, continue the same direction the road ended.
In some variations of the zone the den is a bit to far away from the road, to discover it directly.

In the Semi-Linear Variant the Exit will be West in a direct line from the Crossroads Exit. Place a Portal when you see the exit, to return from later. See Den further away, Semi-Linear Road.

In the Curved Road Variant, the road does a 90° turn West, if you do no't see the Den Entrance, but want to go for it later, place a Portal at the 2nd 90° North-East. When you return to the Zone, walk West to find the Den.
