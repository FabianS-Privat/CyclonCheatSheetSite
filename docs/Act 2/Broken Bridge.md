### Points of Interest

Waypoint  
Abandonend Bandit Camp with Breach  
Exile Jewellery Stash guarded by 3 Rouge Exiles (Hostile after opening Stash)

## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Follow the Road
:::

To find Kraitlin's Bandit Camp, simply follow the Road. When you see the Waypoint you can deviate from the Path, to find in the Left corner of the Broken Castle 3 Rouge Exiles guarding a Jewellery Stash. This is optional, but great for improving Gear.
There is also a Breach, but I would recommend only going for it, if you can see it from the Road.
