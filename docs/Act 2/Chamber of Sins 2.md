## Zone Read

The Fidelitas Boss Arena location can be determined based on the Position and Direction of the Trial.

![Blink Arrow Skip](./img//Chamber%20of%20Sins%202/Blink%20Arrow%20Skip.png)
In the Trial right after entering, the first Section can be skipped by using Blink Arrow or Lightning Warp in the 1st niche. Stand in the Yellow Circle marked Niche, then aim at the Pillar you can see in the distance.

![Final Room](./img/Chamber%20of%20Sins%202/FinalRoomBeforeWalkway.png)
The final Room before the Walkway to the Fidelitas Boss Arena is always the same, it contains 2 Pillars and 1 Lit Fire Basin.

So the first step in Chamber of Sins 2 is locating the Trial. We always head South-East from the Entrance.
If South is a Dead End, we are in the North extending Variant. We find the trial by following the Wall leading to the North-West Corner of the Zone. And the Fidelitas Arena will be in the North-East Corner.

If The Trial is on the East Wall, facing North-East, the Fidelitas Boss Arena will be South and then West from the Trial.

If the Trial is on the Eas Wall, but facing North-West instead. The Fidelitas Boss Arena will be South and then East from the Trial. Carefull to not fall for the Gem Room Dead End which will be either directly connected to the Trial Room Passage or as the first Passage extending East again.

Finally if you don't see a Trial on the east Wall before crossing a Room with 2 Pillars and a lit Fire Basin, in exactly the same Position as the Final Room example above, then both Trial and Exit will be on the West Edge. You will have to move first West, then North-West to find the Trial. After the Trial head South-East till you can move West again, from there go South till you find the Final Room, then head through the Passage to kill Fidelitas.
