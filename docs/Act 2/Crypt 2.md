## Zone Read

![Altar Room](./img/Crypt%202/Final%20Room.png)
The Altar Room is always gona look the Same, big Staircaise leading to the Tombs with the Golden Hand being in the Wall behind it.
There are 4 Variations to Crypt, 2 for the East Facing Starting Room and 2 for the South Facing Starting Room.

If the Starting Room Exit is East, you are either in a North or East Variant. Move East, till you either find the Altar Room or notice that the Zone ends East and forces you to move North.
If the Zone forces you North, move North along the East Wall to get to the Altar Room.

If the Starting Room Exit is South, we follow the same Principle. Move South till the Zone ends and forces you to move West, or you find the Altar Room.
If the Zone forces you West, move West till you find the Altar Room.
