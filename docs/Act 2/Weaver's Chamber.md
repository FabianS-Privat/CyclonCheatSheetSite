## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Follow the Left Wall from the Entrance.
:::

Always go West first at the Entrance, if you can go north, the Zone is the North Variant.
If the Zone doesnt let you go West or South after a bit, you spawned at the South Corner and Weaver will be North-East. (See Image 2)

If you can take a long Row of Rooms or Passage West before a second Row of Rooms or Passage south, Weaver's Arena Entrance will be in the South Corner.

If you can only go West and South for few Rooms, like in Image 1. Take the East Passage when you reach the first Corner that only lets you go East. Weaver will be in the North-East Corner of the Zone.
