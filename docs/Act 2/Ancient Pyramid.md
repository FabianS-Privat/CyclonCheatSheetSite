## Layouts

:::tip Simple Read
Outside the first Floor, the Staircase to the next Floor is always diagonal opposite to the Entrance.
:::

There are always 3 Floors in the Ancient Pyramid.
For the first floor, just like on the Ground Floor in Caverns, Check the Corners. Personally I go counterclockwise, but you can also check them clockwise.
Once you are on the Second Floor, the Staircase up will be in the diagonal opposite Corner. Same for the third Floor.
