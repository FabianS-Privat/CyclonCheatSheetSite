## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Stick to the East Edge.  
If you start at the North Corner, move South.  
Otherwise move North
:::

Oasis either extends North or South. While it is a big open Zone like Vastiri Desert, the Variant can quickly be identified. IF the Zone let's you move North, you are in the North Variant. Otherwise you are at the North Corner and the Sand Pit Boss Arena is in the South Corner.

Once you have identified the direction the Zone extends to head into that direction along the East Edge of the Zone.
