## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Head North-East into the North Corner
:::

Boiling Lake is another Semi Static Layout, while the Exact Zone isn't always the same the Important Location is.

The Basilisk is always in the North Corner, you can spot it based on the Terrain. There are 2 major identifier. First the Stone Statues only spawn in the North Corner and Second the 2 Sulphur Fountains next to the Ledge, forming a _Smiley_ on the Minimap
Additonaly the Zone consist of several Layers, with the Entrance being on the 2nd Layer and the Basilisk being on the 3rd Layer, the Ledge of the _Smiley_ forms a 4th Layer, that slopes towards the Basilisk.
