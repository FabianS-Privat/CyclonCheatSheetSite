### Point of Interest

Supply Hoist, to get from one Layer to the Next

## Zone Read

Descent starts the same as Ascent from Part 1 Act 5, however the Bridge is ripped and we have to descent instead. The Zone consist of 3 Layers connected by Supply Hoists.

The first Layer is always Static and the Exit is North-East of the Entrance along the West Edge of the first Layer.

The 2nd and 3rd Layer are either both vertical or both Horizontal. You can determin which variant you are in the moment you exit the Supply Hoist.

- If the 2nd Layer starts in a East Tunnel you are in the Vertical Zone Variant and you move South-East to find the Supply Hoist to the next Layer or Exit.

- If the 2nd Layer starts in a West Tunnel, you are in the Horizontal Variant and you move in the 2nd Layer South-West into North-West after crossing a Bridge. And East / South-East into North-East to find the Exit.
