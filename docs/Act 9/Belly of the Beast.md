## Zone Read

Belly of the Beast is a Series of long twisted Tunnels. It is one of the biggest Zones in Path of Exile 1, based on Minimap Size. To identify which variant you are in there are 2 Identifier.

- Only one Layout enters from the North and has no Square in the first Section. If you have the Entrance from North the Zone will take a Curve East. Follow the Zone till the first Split of North-West. Follow the Split Path till you find a Cross Section, head North-West again till the Path Splits of West, then follow the West Tunnel Westwards till you see the Exit.

All other Variants have a Square and Entrance is from the East. You know that you entered the Square at the first Split.

| Enter from the North, Path Splits West and South                                                 | Enter from West, Path Splits East and South                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| ![Split Path Square Variant 1](<./img/Belly%20of%20the%20Beast/Split%20Path%20Square%20(1).png>) | ![Split Path Square Variant 2](<./img/Belly%20of%20the%20Beast/Split%20Path%20Square%20(2).png>) |

- If you enter the Square from the North, take the West Path, in 2/3 Cases it is the correct Path and the Exit to the Square is in the Left Corner either North or East, however the rest of the Zone is the exact same Read. Follow the Path till you can move North, then move North till you can move East. Finally follow the Path till you can move North again, then move North till the Exit

- If the Left Corner of the Square has no Exit follow the Square till you find the Exit South, then follow the Path South till it splits of West, take the West Tunnel till you can move North. Follow the Path North to find the Exit.

If you enter the Square from the West, always go straight East.

- If the Square exits in the Top Right Corner east, go North once you can till you can split of West or reach the Cross Section. In any other Layout this would be the Tripple Dead End, but in this one (if no Split West happend before) the West Path is the Correct one. Follow that Tunnel till you can move North again. Take the Norht Tunnel till it splits East and the East Split will lead to the Exit.

![Cross Section](./img/Belly%20of%20the%20Beast/Split%20Path.png)

- Finaly if the Square exits in the Bottom Right Corner follow the Tunnel till the zone curves North. Don't take the first Split North, wait for the Curve. After the Curve follow the Path, if it splits in a T-Section take the West Path and continue to follow the Path North till it splits West. Take the West Curve and follow the Path till you find the Exit. If it splits/ has a Crosssection one more Time, take the North Split.
