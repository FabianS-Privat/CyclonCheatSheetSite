## Progression

| Present                                         | Past                                      |

Act 3 Progression is Split between Past and Present. Just like in Act 1 the Progression can be split into 4 Sections.  
The <span className="red-text"> red Arrows</span> are for the Arrival Section.  
The <span className="orange-text"> orange Arrows</span> are for the Vaal Section.  
The <span className="blue-text"> blue Arrows</span> are for the Drowned City Section.  
The <span className="yellow-text"> yellow Arrows</span> are for the Past Section.

White / Black Text is required for Main Story Progression or Permanent Buffs. <br/>
<span className="red-text"> Red Text</span> marks Optional Tasks for additional Uncut Skill Gems or Uncut Support Gems, the Notes include the Reward.

Several Tasks in one Area can be completed in any Order, unless otherwise noted.
