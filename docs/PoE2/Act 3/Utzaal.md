# Utzaal

## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Follow the Road!
:::

Utzaal is the prime Version of Drowned City. While the rules of Drowned City can be applied here, it is way simpler to just follow the Road and take Turns towards the Ambush / Viper Arena when the Road Forks.
After the Viper Fight check if Bottom Right is a Dead End, if it isn't follow it.

```mermaid
graph TD;
A[Follow the Road]-->|Road forks, but no down|B[take the Right Path]
B-->A
A[Follow the Road]-->|Road forks|D[take the down Path]
D-->A
A-->|Viper Fight|C[Check if Bottom Right is Dead End]
C-->|Dead End|E[Continue to follow Road upwards]
C-->|Continues|G[Follow Road downwards]
```
