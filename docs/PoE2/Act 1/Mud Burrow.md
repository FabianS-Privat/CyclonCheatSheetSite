# Mud Burrow

## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
The Devourer Chamber is always at the opposite End of the Zone.
The Zone either extends Horizontal or Vertical. Follow the Left Edge till you reach the Devourer.
:::

```mermaid
graph TD;
a[Tunnel Moving South]-->|yes|b[Zone is Vertical, move Down, at Crossroads, always go Down if able. Otherwise stick to Left Edge]
a-->|no|c[Zone is Horizontal, move Left, at Crossroads, always go Left if able. Otherwise stick to Upper Edge]
```
