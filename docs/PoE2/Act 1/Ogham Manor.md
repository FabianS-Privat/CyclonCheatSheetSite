# Ogham Manor

## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
There is no true simple read for this zone. The Quest Items are placed roughly in a triangle, usually first on the Starting Side of the River and the 2nd and 3rd on the other.  
Accept that this zone might take a bit and just focus on efficient killing as you search for the Obelisks.
:::
