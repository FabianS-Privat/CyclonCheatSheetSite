# Tomb of the Consort

## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Follow the Edge of the tiny Alcove at Spawn, about 80% accurate to find the Boss Room.
:::

Tomb of the Consort has many Variants, but they can be sorted into 4 Categories.

- Dead End
- Early Ambush
- Reverse
- No Indicator

There are also the Inner Boss room Variants which are a Subcategorie of either Early Ambush or no Indicator Categories.

Tomb of the Consort has very linear smooth Walls, this Pattern is only broken for the two Point of Interest. This is even more visible when having Landscape Transparency not on 0.

Due to many different Variants the Flowchart is a bit convoluted, consider Zooming in with 'CTRL + Mouse Wheel Forward' to 200%

```mermaid
graph TD;
A[Check Both Sides of the T Section]-->|Either Side is a Dead-End|B[Follow the Other Path, stick to the Dead End Edge at Cross Sections]
B-->|Found Boss Room|C[Look for viable Dead Ends for the Ambush on the Minimap and check those]
B-->|Found Ambush|D[If you do not see the Boss Room Checkpoint or Enviroment head for the opposite Far Corner of the Zone]
A-->|Ambush Checkpoint at either Side|E[Follow the other Path]
E-->|Area behind Beginning T Section is empty & both Paths continue|F[Likely to be inner Boss Room, continue the Path, but look for Boss Room Tile Set in the Center]
E-->|Area behind Beginning T Section isn't empty or Early Ambush is a Dead End |G[Head to the Diagonal Opposite Far Corner of the Ambush]
A-->|Ambush Checkpoint at either Side & Path continues paralel to Entrance|H[Return to Entrance Checkpoint & Follow opposite Path of Ambush to find Boss Room]
A-->|Both Path continue & no Ambush is visible|I[Follow the Path that leads in the same Direction as the small Apendex near the Waypoint]
I-->|Area behind Beginning T Section is empty|F
```
