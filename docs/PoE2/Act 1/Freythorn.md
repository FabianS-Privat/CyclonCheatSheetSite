# Freythorn

## Zone Read

:::danger No proper Read
There is no true Read for this Zone, best we can do is a small Circle around the Waypoint and then a big Circle. Hoping we find all 4 Ritual Sites.
:::

Freythorn is a unsolved Zone, there is a 1/12 Chance to get a very good Layout with all Rituals close to the Entrance. Otherwise it is gona be finding 3 Needles in a Haystack.

2 minor Things that slightly help with the search.

1. The small Rituals are always on a square or rectangular Tile. Never on a Pathway or Circle Tile.
2. If you find the big Ritual before the small Rituals. Black smoke swirls split of the big Ritual in the rough direction of the small ones.
