# Halani Gates

## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Follow the Inner Edge of the Zone.
:::

Halani Gates is a Semi-Static Layout, a Set of 3 closed Gates. At the end of the Zone Jamanra awaits with a unwinnable Boss Fight. It will end when Jamanra gets to 1/3 HP after a short Dialog.

Following the inner Edge of the Zone, as in the Edge closer to the Valley, is the shortest Path from one Gate to the next. Opposite to the 2nd Gate is a Outpost with a Checkpoint and a Rare Miniboss. If Asala got stuck, you can respawn at Checkpoint to force summon her to you.

After the Jamanra Fight, take the Stairs down and move into the same direction the Zone has been extending, to trigger the Quest State for the Main Quest.
