# Lightless Passage

## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Well of Souls always in Top Left Corner of Zone
:::

Lightless Passage is a Semi-Static Layout. There is only 1 Variant but the Enemy Wave Encounter at the fake Well can be in 2 different Locations & the Tunnels are randomly generated.

The Exit is always in the far opposite Corner to the Entrance, right now all Layouts generated place the Entrance in the Bottom Right Corner and Exit to Well of Souls in the Top Left. Though theoretically the Layout could be mirrored.

:::info Cyclon's Note
Unless you want to use Desecrate Crafting during Campaign, you can also just get a Guild Mate or Buddy to give you Well of Souls Waypoint and completly skip this Zone.
:::
