# Valley of the Titans

## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Follow either Edge till you found all PoI
:::

Valley of the Titans is a solved Zone with 12 Variants. The Variant can be distinguisehd by the Entrance Orientation and the Length of the Entrance Halway. There is a solved Solution for each Variant you can find [here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vTy_NGG7OyZZS_HNhtIf9FcEFgYN0JcPQ5vhzszXWnUE1N2i8FRQyj3gI69WkpzI3B3QoPLdS01wjpv/pub) from Lundburgerr or on the Campaign Codex Discord.

Currently the Zone does not rotate or gets mirrored. So a static reading approach is viable. All 12 Variants can appear regardless of the World Seed.

The zone is roughly a big O with an outer Edge and an inner Edge. The Seals and Waypoint with the Medallion Pedestal are usualy along the outer Edges but in some Variants they can be on the inner Edge of the Zone.

The Waypoint and Medalion Pedestal are always connected by a small Tunnel to the rest of the Map.

Props to Crimson Cast for the Cheat Sheet made based on Lundburgerr Discovery.
![Valley of Titan Cheat Sheet](./img/Valley%20of%20the%20titans/Valley_of_Titans.jpg)

- Yellow Diamonds are seals.
- Blue is Grotto Entrance.
- Purple Triangle is the Waypoint.
- If there's a four point star, the entrance path is long.
- If you're in the curvy arrow variant, you will know.

Due to the existance of the visual Guide and the fact there are 12 Variants I will skipp writing a convoluted long Graph for now.
