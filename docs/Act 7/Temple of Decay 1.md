## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
First Floor: Follow the Zone.  
Second Floor: Exit is Diagonal Opposite End to the Entrance.
:::

Temple of Decay 1 has 2 Floors, the first Floor is a direct Path with some Dead Ends splitting of. Move South-East till you enter a Big Square Platfrom, from there only 1 Bridge leads further. Walk in the direction the Bridge forces you till you hit a Corner, then move in the direction the Corner forces you into.

The 2nd Floor is in the Shape of an 8 or Infinity Symbol. Choose either Wall and follow along the Outer Edge of the Zone till you find the Entrance to Temple of Decay 2.
