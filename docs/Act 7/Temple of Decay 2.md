## Zone Read

Temple of Decay 2 has 3 Floors + Boss Room. Floor 1 and 2 behave like the 2nd Floor of Temple of Decay 1, with the Exception that 2 of the 4 Bridges on the Big Zentral Platform are broken.  
There are always 2 opposing intact Bridges and 2 opposing broken Bridges. Flame Dash / Blink Arrow / Leap Slam / Lightning Warp can dash across the broken Bridges.

The direction the Stair faces is the Direction with the intact Bridge. Move in that Direction till you reach a Corner, then turn and move the Direction the Zone extends to, to cross the 1st Bridge.  
If you do not have one of the above mentioned Movement Skills, walk straigth through the Platform to cross the 2nd intact Bridge and once hitting a corner turn to find the Staircase to the next Floor.
If you have a Dash Skill that can cross the broken Bridges, it is faster to dash over the Broken Bridge direct next to the one you just crossed. Then repeat the same Directions you moved to get to the first Bridge.
