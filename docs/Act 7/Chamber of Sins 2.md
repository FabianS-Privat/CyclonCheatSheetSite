## Zone Read

:::warning Not like the Act 2 Version
The Chamber of Sins in Act 7 do not follow the same Pattern as the Chamber of Sins 2 of Act 2.
:::

Walkway and Exit to Den can be determined based on the Trial Position and the first couple Rooms.

- If the South Room at the Start is a Dead End, move in a North West North Pattern through the Rooms till you find the Trial. From there stick to the West Edge to find the Walkway.
- If the Room below the Start Room forces you to path West then ends in another Corner that forces North, the Trial is along the West Edge and the Exit is in the North Corner. Follow the North Edge, but pass the First Room North.
- If the Zone extends South walk through the Rooms till you reach the Pillar and Basin Room

![Pillar and Basin Room](./img/Chamber%20of%20Sins%202/Trial%20Visible.png)

- - If you can see the Trial the Walkway is in the South Corner of the Zone
- - If you can not see the Trial, the Trial is West of the Room and the Walkway is in the East Corner, but the connection goes over the South Corner.

### Fancy Skip

This skip is more fancy than efficient, it saves a couple Seconds at most.
Stand at the broken Table and aim above the broken Gate with Blink Arrow / Lightning Warp / Leap Slam.
