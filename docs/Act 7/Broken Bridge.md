## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Follow the Road
:::

The Road connects the Town Entrance with the Exit to Crossroads. The Road always starts facing North and will take several Curves East or West before turning East and then taking Curves North or South. Along the Road you can find the Broken Waypoint.

- While the Road is going North, the broken Waypoint can appear East of the Road
- Otherwise once the Road is going East the Waypoint can appear North or South of the Road

Behind the broken Waypoint is always the Castle Ruins containing the Dirty Lockbox for the Flask Sidequest.
