## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Move along the West Edge
:::

Causeway has two major Variants where one has 2 Sub Variants.

Move along the West Edge North-East, if the Zone curves East before you can move North again, you are in the Curved Variant. Stick to the now Inner Edge and move West once available. After the Waypoint move South whenever you can, otherwise West.

If the Zone lets you move North-East till you reach the Waypoint, continue North-East along the West Edge.
