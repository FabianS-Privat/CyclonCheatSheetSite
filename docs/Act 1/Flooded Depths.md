## Zone Read

The Zone opens up in any one of the cardinal Directions: North, East, South, or West.

You can identify in which direction based on the Directions the Zone ends in.

As Example in Image 1 the Zone ends South-East and South-West, you are forced to path towards North.
Or in Image 2 where the Zone ends West and you see a bit of Passages going both North or South. So the Zone has to go East and all North and South Passages will lead to Dead Ends.

You can draw a Arrow on the Zone in the Directions the Layout ends, and in the opposite will be the Boss.
