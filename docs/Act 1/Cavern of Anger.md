## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read

Follow the Water

:::

The Exit of the Zone is always in the North-East Corner of the Zone, but the correct pathing is either mainly north or mainly east.

To determine the correct pathing, check the upper Bridge of the 2 wooden Bridges at the Start of the Zone. If the Bridge leads into a Dead End. The Zone extends north and you should mainly path North to find the shortest Route to the Boss Arena.
If the Zone extends after the Bridge and leads east. You mainly path east to find the shortest Path to the Boss Arena.

The rule of following the Water / River still applies.
