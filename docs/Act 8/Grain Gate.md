## Zone Read

Grain Gate consist of several Zones and Indoor Areas connecting them. While most Indoor Areas are Dead Ends, some connect one Zone to the next one. These are highlighted by Corpses next to them.

If you enter the Zone from the West of the Waypoint, the first Indoor Passage is North of the WP and both the Hidden Underbelly Entrance and Gemling Legion are in the 2nd Zone.

If you enter the Zone south of the Waypoint, the first Indoor Passage is West of the Waypoint and the Gemling Legion is in the 3rd Zone while the Hidden Underbelly Entrance is in the 2nd Zone.

The Gemling Legion can be pinpointed by the Book Icon on the Minimap.
![Book in Fog](./img/Grain%20Gate/Book%20in%20Fog.png)

After the Gemling Legion is dead we want to search for the Underbelly Entrance. It can be anywhere in the 2nd Zone of the Area.
You can spot it based on the Crane next to it and the Ground covered by rusted Metal.
