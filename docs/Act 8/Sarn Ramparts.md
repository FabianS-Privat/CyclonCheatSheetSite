## Zone Read

Sarn Ramparts has 2 Variants, however we read and run them exactly the same.  
Move East till you hit the Rampart Wall, continue East along the Wall. At the Corner move North along the Rampart Wall. Enter the top of the Wall at the broken Wall Section.  
Walk on top of the Wall till you see a Staircase going down. At the Staircase move West/East to enter Town.
