## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Follow the Carpet
:::

Lunaris Temple 1 in Part 2 is a cleaned up Version of its Part 1 Variant.

Follow the Carpet North-East till you get to the big Room with the Rows of Banks. From there the Carpet either Splits of East or West. Follow it until the next big Room.

- If the Carpet splits of East. Head North-East, while sticking to the West Edge

- If the Carpet splits of West. Head North-West, while sticking to the West Edge, with the Exception of the first Door West.
