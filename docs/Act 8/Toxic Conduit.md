## Zone Read

The Sewer Door at the Entrance is either open towards East or West.
The Zone can only extend East if the Gate opens West, but could always be East regardless if the Sewer Door swings East or West.

Do one of the two

- Always go opposite the Direction the Door opend.
- Or always go West first as there are 2 West Variants.

After you have found the direction the Zone extends to, always move North if you can, if you can not move North, move the direction the Zone extends to.

:::tip Cyclon's Note
I have not tested which one is overall better in 100+ Runs. Statisticly both are a 2/3, but not all Layouts feel as common.
:::
