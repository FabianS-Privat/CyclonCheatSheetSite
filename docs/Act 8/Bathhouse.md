## Zone Read

Similar to Solaris Concourse in Act 8 we are entering Bathhouse via the Hidden Underbelly, placing us at a initialy unknown Point in the Zone. However we can quickly identify where we are at.
To reenter the Zone place a Portal in the 1st Room after the Entrance.

- If our Starting Room is facing East, we are in the South Spawn, the Exit is straight North-East and the Trial is in the North-West Corner. We have to travel through the Middle Room with Hector Titucius to get there. From the Trial the Exit to High Gardens is in the North Corner.
- If our Starting Room is facing West, we are in the North-East Spawn. The Exit is South along the East Edge from the Room and the Trial is North. The Exit to High Gardens is West from the Trial along the North Edge.
- Otherwise our Room is facing South and we need a 2nd Indicator to identify the Layout.
- - If we see the Levers of the Trial East of the Starting Room we are in the 2nd North-East Variant. Exit to Lunaris Concourse is South-East and the Exit to High Gardens is West.
- - If we see the Hector Quest Indicator when moving South, we are in the North-West Variant. The Exit to Lunaris Concourse is East. The Trial is West and the Exit to High Gardens is North through the Hector Main Room.
- - Finally if we have no Indicator the Layout is the South Spawn and behaves like the East Facing Room South Spawn, with the Exception that we can turn West beforfrom the Portal to safe couple Seconds.
