## Zone Read

Daresso's Dream can be a bit Tricky, but there does exist a Simple Read.

:::tip Simple Read
If you can move East move East, otherwise move South till you can move East Again, when you hit the East Edge, move North.
:::

With a few Exceptions, the Simple Read does get you through the Zone.

The first Exception is the U Pattern Variant. If the First and Second Room both only have 1 Exit, then in the Third Room move West. This is the only Variant where the Exit is West.

In any other Variant the Exit is in the North Corner of the Zone at the East Edge. We move North till we hit the First Room, from there we move East till we hit the East Edge or a 2 Path Room, on the 2 Path Room always go South, then resume moving East.

The Last Exception is the Maze into Maze Variant, where we have to walk into a Dead End before realizing we reached the East Edge, move back out of it then turn North.
