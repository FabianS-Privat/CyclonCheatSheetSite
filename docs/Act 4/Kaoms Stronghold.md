## Zone Read

Kaom's Stronghold similar to Kaom's Dream is a _linear_ Zone. Again as in only has 1 Path to take that you want to follow. Almost all Variants can be summarized in the Snaking Pattern.  
However if the Big Rock you walk past is on the East Side then you can walk straigth North. This is the only variant that spawns a second Staircase to the 2nd Layer.
