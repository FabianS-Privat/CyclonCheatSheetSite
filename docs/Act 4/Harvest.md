## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
This Zone is a Static Layout, the only random Part in this Zone is which Boss is where.
One Boss is always East of the Waypoint and two Bosses are West of the Waypoint.
:::

The only thing to add to the Simpel Read here is that Logging for American Servers or Portaling for others after Killing Boss 1 and Boss 3 is faster than walking. It also refreshes your Flasks.
