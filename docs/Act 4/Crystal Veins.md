## Zone Read

The Final Room always has 2 Entrances, one from the South and one from the West. The Zone always has a long Path and a Short Path to the Entrances of the Final Room. To find the Shortest Path we differentiate between North and South Variants.

For the South Variant there is a simple Read

:::tip Simple Read
Stick to the opposite Edge of the Direction the first Passage takes.
:::

To extend on that Simple Read, if the First Passage goes West, we want to stick to the East Edge, avoiding Dead Ends. We can assume if a Passage is a Dead End East, by knowing that we have to walk till the South Edge before turning East.
But if the First Passage goes East, we want to stick to the West Edge and take the first Tunnel West to find the Exit.

For the North Variant we can determine the Sub Variant based on the First couple Rooms. If the First Rooms do a almost Loop and we end up in a Room right above the Entrance, stick to the West Edge and move North till you find the South Entrance to the Final Room.
If the first Couple Rooms only go South, move West once you able to and only start moving North once you can hit the West Edge.
To differentiate between the 2nd North Variant and the South Variants. We know that the North Variants takes at least 2 Rooms South, while the South Variants always go 1 Room South and then curve either West or East.
