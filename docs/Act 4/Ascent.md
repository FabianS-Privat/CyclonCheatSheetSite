### Point of Interest

Lever to activate the Portal

## Zone Read

Move North, in the middle of the Zone is a long Bridge. After the Bridge stick to the East Edge till you get past the Encampent with the Crafting Recipe.
When the Path curves West, move West to activate the Lever and enter the Slave Pens.
