### Point of Interest

Deshret's Spirit

## Zone Read

Mines 2 can be determined based on the Layout and the general Path the Zone takes.  
If the Zone extends South, follow the Path till you see Deshret Spirit, free here, then continue.

- Deshret might be in a Dead End East or South. Just Follow the Path along the Outer Edge of the Zone till you see the Quest Icon.
  Knowing roughly where the Exit is helps with avoiding Dead Ends. If you hit the South Edge without finding the Exit, then the Exit is in the East Corner.

If the Zone extends North, follow the Path, if the Zone quickly ends South and you can only move North stick ot the East Edge of the Zone to find Deshret.

If the Zone instead continues South for a Bit, stick to the South Edge till you find Deshret, then stick to the West Edge.
