## Zone Read

The Exit to Chamber of Innocence is paralel to the Entrance from Oriath Square. To get to the Exit we follow the Inner Wall of the Zone East till we get to the _Courtroom_. After that we move east till we get to the same height as the Entrance andm ove North to find the Exit to Chamber of Innocence.
