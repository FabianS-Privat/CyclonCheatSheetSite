## Zone Read

Control Blocks has 2 Variants, we can quickly deduce which Variant we are in at the Start. In the 2 Vertical Trench Variant we are forced to move a U Pattern at the Start, while the 1 Horizontal Trench Variant has a Series of Room moving North.

In the 2 Vertical Trench Variant we want to take the first opportunity moving East after the U Pattern. Dash into the Trench and walk out at the Stairs East.  
Then take the next opportunity to go East again to be able to move North and collect the Miasmeter. From there we move East till we reach the 2nd Trench. Walk the Stairs down and dash out at the broken Fence.  
Continue South till we have another opportunity to move east. Kill Casticus and follow the Path to reach the exit.

In the 1 Horizontal Trench Variant we move North till we are forced to enter the first trench to continue. Exit it directly with a Dash and continue North to collect the Miasmeter. From there we can move through the Zone similar to the 2 Vertical Trenches Variant with the exception of entering the 2nd Trench from the North.  
The 1 Horizontal Trench Variant has one noticable Skip, if you do not need the Miasmeter or prefer to collect it later. You can dash through the Wall and Blockage at the inner Curve End of it with a Blink Skill.

### Broken Gate Skip

Use any Blink Skill at a Broken Gate to Dash through. Works both up and down.

### Blockage Skip

Position yourself directly under the Gap.  
If its Horizontal into Vertical Trench its at the West Side and if its Vertical into Horizontal Trench its at the East Side.
Aim slightly above your player above the BLockage (still inside the Yellow Circle) to dash over it.
