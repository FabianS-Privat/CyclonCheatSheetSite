### Points of Interest

## Zone Read

Ruined Square is a static Layout, it will always look the same.

To get from the Entrance to the Waypoint, follow the Zone in a S Pattern.

The Bloodfountain is in a straight West Passage from the Waypoint.

North-East of the Bloodfountain is the Rooftop Entrance

South of the Bloodfountian you will always find the Reliquary Entrance.
