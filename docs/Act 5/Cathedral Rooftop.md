## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Move North-East
:::

In the Act 5 Variant of Cathedral Rooftop, normaly you can always move through the Center of the Rooftop north-east.  
Very Rarely the Zone generates in a Way you have to take the Walkway east or west if the middle is blocked.
