## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Choose Clockwise or Counterclockwise, Run the Zone along the Outer Edge in the choosen direction.
:::

The Quest Items are in the North, East and South Corner of the Zone. the middle of the Zone is not directly crossable. You have to move along the Edges.
The Vault leads to a Room with a Chest contianing a couple Rare Items and Gold Piles(Random Loot), to open the Vault you have to find 2 Keys hidden in a random White Chest in the Zone, this is not worth your time. Recommended to ignore.

:::tip Cyclon's Note
After the Third Quest Item, move back to the Waypoint or Die to Respawn in Town(Softcore only)
:::
