## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Always stick to the Outer Wall of the Zone
:::

Chamber of Innocence has a somewhat Static Layout, they Key Difference between the Variants is in the East Part of the Zone. When the Rooms connect South to the Boss Arena. Sometimes you can move directly South, sometimes the Outer Edge of the Zone leads you past the first direct Path and you enter the Final Room before the Boss Chamber from the South or West.

AFter killing Innocence a 2nd Exit opens that places you near the Waypoint.

:::tip Cyclon's Note
Each Elevated Section of this Zone contains a guranteed Blue Pack. Great Zone to powerlevel or catch up on Experience. Up to Level 41 on a Solo Run.
:::
