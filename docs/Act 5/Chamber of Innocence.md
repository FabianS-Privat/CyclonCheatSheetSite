## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Always stick to the Outer Wall of the Zone
:::

Chamber of Innocence has a somewhat Static Layout, they Key Difference between the Variants is in the East Part of the Zone. When the Rooms connect South to the Boss Arena.

You can identify when to move South based on the first elevated Section that cuves South. If the only way to continue is the Passage South, head South to find the Boss Room. Otherwise move West till you get the next Passage South, then that is the Correct Passage to the Boss Room.

After killing Innocence a 2nd Exit opens that places you near the Waypoint.

:::tip Cyclon's Note
Each Elevated Section of this Zone contains a guranteed Blue Pack. Great Zone to powerlevel or catch up on Experience. Up to Level 41 on a Solo Run.
:::
