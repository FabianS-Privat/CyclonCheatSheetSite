## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Walk forward till you hit a Wall and see the Zone is not further Extending in that direction. Turn in the direction the Zone is extending to and walk forward again.
If you hit a Wall, but see the Zone is further extending in that direction, walk around the Wall and walk further forward.
:::

Follow the Simple Read till you get to the Waypoint. based on the Waypoint Position, or rather the Corner it is in, we can determine the general Direction of the Exit. If the Corner of the Waypoint is pointing East. the Exit is in the South-East Corner.
If the Waypoint is pointing North The Exit is either in the North Corner or North-West Corner, check the North Corner first, otherwise move North-West to find the Exit to Solaris Temple 2.
