### Point of Interest

Caged Scion (First Playthrough only)

## Zone Read

The upper Sceptre of God follows the same Layout Rules as the Sceptre of God, with some additions.

The Side-Passages or Cornes can be replaced with Balconies. A Corner Balconie can never be the Corner containing the Staircase to the next Floor.  
The Upper floor is a lot more rundown, sometimes there are broken Walls, if two of those align or one and the Room after has a railing, it can be dashed through with a Lvl 10 Movement Skill.

The Third Floor is a Static Layout, you always walk to the center then move North-East past the Cage to find the Entrance to the Rooftop.
