### Points of Interest

4 Golden Pages

## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Traverse the Zone Clockwise
:::

Each Golden Page is in a seperate Alcove filled with Books. This Zone is semi-static. You have a set amount of potential Positions the Golden Pages can spawn in. The Zone is a Square that in the North-West Corner does not connect.
It is best to always move clockwise through the Zone and recommended to check the East corner, even if the Path does not directly connect to it.  
When you get close to an Alcove, if it does contain a Golden Page, the Quest Marker will appear. So there is no need to go into Dead End Alcoves if you do not see a Quest Marker in them.
