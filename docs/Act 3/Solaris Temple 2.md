## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Follow the Red Carpet
:::

The Zone is going in one of the 4 cardinal Directions. Based on the Direction the Zone expands too, we can very Quickly pinpoint Diallas Position.
One thing to note is that the Final Room always has the same Layout and can only be entered from the South-West or South-East. This helps figuring out where the correct Path has to be.
