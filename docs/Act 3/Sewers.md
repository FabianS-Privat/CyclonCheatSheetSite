## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Big Room after WP, 3rd Bust Left of Exit, Small Room after WP, 3rd Bust South.
:::

Leading up to the Waypoint the Zone is fairly Linear, with 1 Deadend we have to check. It can either contain the Bust or be just a Deadend.
After the Waypoint we can easily read the Zone.

- If the Room behind the Bust is a big open Room, the 3rd Bust will be West of the Exit.

- If the Room behind the Bust is a Linear Coridor and South of the WP is the 2nd Bust, the third Bust will be in the South-East Corner, with a Connection to the North. Where you can get to the Exit.

- If the 2nd Bust is in a Small Room, after a Cross Corridor. The 3rd Bust should be collected first, it will be in a Deadend Room in the South-East Corner.
