## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Follow one Edge till you find the Trial
:::

The Ossuary itself is filled with Skulls & Bones. Instead the Passage to the Bone Pits is now open. Bonepits has 4 Variants. Each Variant is either a 8-Shape or a Infinity-Shape. We can differentiate the Variants based on the Starting Location, but can't identify if it is a 8-Shape or Infinity-Shape until after the first Curve.

- If you start facing East you are on the West Edge. Move North-East till you reach the first corner, move South-East till you get to the Big Room. From there move North-East to find the Trial.
- - If instead of reaching the Big Room you are forced to move South-West, continue to follow the Zone to reach the Big Room. From there stick to the East Edge South to find the Trial. You should be seeing the Lever Icon on the Minimap when forced to move South-West.

- If you start facing West, you are on the West Edge. Move South-West till you reach the first corner, move South-East till you get to the Big Room. From there move South-West to find the Trial.
- - If instead of reaching the Big Room you are force to move North-East, continue to follow the Zone to reach the Big Room, cross it and continue to move North-East till you reach the Corner, from there move South-East to find the Trial.
