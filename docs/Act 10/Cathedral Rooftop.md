## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Rescue Bannon North of the Entrance, Exit is South-East Corner. Always head South East.
:::

Cathedral Rooftop is a Semi Static Zone, the main layout is the same, but sometimes the Middle of the Rooftop is blocked and you have to walk East on a Wooden Scaffold.

Bannon is always right next to the Entrance in the former Kitava Boss Fight Arena.
The Exit is always in the South-East Corner. Move South-East and stay in the Middle of the Roof. If the Path is Blocked like in Image 2 above, look for the Wooden Scaffolding East and take the Passage.
