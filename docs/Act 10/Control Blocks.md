## Zone Read

:::tip Simple Read
Move along the North Edge West. The Boss Arena Entrance is in the North-West Corner
:::

Control Blocks is a reverse Run of the Act 5 Version. The Entrance is in the North-East Corner and the Boss Arena Entrance is in the North-West Corner. While it is possible to just follow the North Edge. It is faster to Dash down in the first Trench and use its Direction (Horizontal or Vertical) to identify the Layout.

- If the Trench is Horizontal follow it till it ends. In the Top West Corner should be a broken Gate, Dash up and continue North-West to find the Boss Arena.

- If the Trench is Vertical, Dash Down at the first Chance and move North-West along the Trench, Dash back up on the West Edge when possible and continue West when you can. The Boss Arena will be in the North-West Corner, thought sometimes it spawns a bit further out.
